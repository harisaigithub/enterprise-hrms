## 🏷️ Milestone & Feature Breakdown

### 1. Expense Management & File Handling (October 2026)
- **Draft Flow & Local Fallback:** Added local storage fallback for MinIO receipt uploads/downloads and complete draft save/edit capabilities.
- **Validation & RBAC:** Resolved permission strings and updated frontend modal submission lifecycle.

### 2. DevOps, Kubernetes & Observability (Late September 2026)
- **Deployment & Scaling:** Kubernetes deployments, HPA autoscaling, ingress/load balancing, and microservices configuration.
- **Telemetry Stack:** Full observability instrumentation with Prometheus, Grafana, Loki, and Jaeger.

### 3. Core Enterprise Workflows (Mid to Late September 2026)
- **Payroll System:** Enhanced Form-16 download, payslip PDF generation, PII data masking/encryption, and dual-level approval & release workflows.
- **Attendance & Leave:** Attendance regularization workflow, break persistence, and leave decision rules.
- **Employee & ESS:** Hierarchy-based RBAC, bulk employee import, candidate onboarding flow, and employee self-service (ESS) dashboard.
- **Security & RBAC:** Fine-grained access control, audit logging, and Linux service hardened import paths.
